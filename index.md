# Ledger privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

**Ledger collects no data.** It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no accounts. Nothing you enter into Ledger is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

**Where your data lives.** Ledger stores what you enter (bills, income, budgets, transactions and settings) on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, Ledger also syncs it through your own private iCloud account, so it is available on your other devices signed into the same Apple ID. Only you can access that data: it sits in your private area of iCloud, which the developer cannot see or read. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to iCloud.

**Backups and exports.** If you choose a backup folder or export a file, Ledger writes a copy of your data to the place you chose. Ledger doesn't send it anywhere else.

**Deleting your data.** Deleting Ledger from an iPhone or iPad removes its data from that device. To remove your data from iCloud too, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage (on a Mac: System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage), choose Ledger, and delete its data.

**TestFlight.** If you use a test version through TestFlight, Apple may share with the developer the information you choose to send as feedback (such as screenshots and your email address) and basic information such as crashes. That is covered by Apple's TestFlight terms.
